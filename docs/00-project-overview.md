# Life Gamify (Coin Rewards)
### A personal gamification platform for building habits through a Coin Economy and Reward Store.

---

## Vision

The goal of this project is to create a highly motivating personal habit tracker that rewards completed activities with **virtual Coins (🪙)**, which can be spent in a customizable **Reward Store**.

Instead of fixed single rewards, habits earn **Coins**, and users unlock real-world guilty pleasures or treats by purchasing them from their personal **Reward Store**.

Example Store Items:
- **Bourbon Packet**: 12 coins
- **Doomscrolling (15 min)**: 5 coins
- **Coffee / Chai Break**: 8 coins
- **Gaming Hour**: 20 coins
- **Movie / Episode**: 25 coins

The application should feel rewarding, playful, and deeply satisfying with dynamic themes and customizable celebration effects.

---

## Primary Goal

Increase consistency in healthy habits by converting abstract routine tasks into a tangible **Coin Economy** with instant store gratification.

Instead of:
+10 generic points

The app gives:
+3 Coins 🪙 -> Unlocks 15 min Doomscrolling or a Bourbon packet in your Store!

---

## Core Philosophy

This application is built for personal motivation and long-term consistency.

Everything is optimized around:
- High motivation & instant feedback
- Flexible Reward Store (custom prices, custom items, image uploads)
- Visual delight, dynamic color themes, and celebratory FX
- Low friction 1-tap habit logging
- Zero infrastructure cost
